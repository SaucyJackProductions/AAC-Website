## Platform

## Users

Primary clients, confirmed as the current focus:

- **Golf courses and clubs:** courses, clubs and resorts that want aerial photography, cinematic flyovers and playable 3D course models, so golfers see the course before they book a tee time.
- **Real estate and rentals:** agents, brokerages, developments, vacation rentals and property managers who need listing films, twilight aerials, walkthroughs and agent-branded reels that help win listings, defend price and fill booked nights.

Secondary audiences the site still serves: hospitality and dining (restaurants, resorts, hotels, charters) and local businesses buying social and paid creative.

The visitor usually arrives deciding whether to hire a premium local production studio. Many arrive in person, by scanning Chandler's QR code or opening his digital card after meeting him.

## Product Purpose

Above All Media is a cinematic film, licensed aerial and social content studio. It makes a client's business look like the market leader, and then turns that attention into calls, bookings, listings and higher-paying clients. Success for the website is a qualified inquiry: a completed two-step project form, a call or text to Chandler, or a saved contact.

## Positioning

Goal-first production. Every project starts from the business result it has to deliver (calls, bookings, listings, reach) and is planned and cut backward from that. The work is shot by a licensed drone operator who lives in the markets he films. The studio also builds interactive 3D experiences (Sky Caddy, Hole 10, Hilton Head Island), which a typical local videographer cannot offer.

## Product Principles

1. **Results before reels.** Every surface connects the craft to a business outcome a golf course or listing agent cares about.
2. **Show the actual footage.** Real aerial and cinematic work is the proof. Lead with it, and never pad it with invented claims.
3. **Personal, not agency.** Inquiries go to Chandler directly. The path to talking to him stays short and visible everywhere.
4. **Two home markets, one standard.** The Lowcountry and Knoxville get the same level of craft and confidence.
5. **Something competitors can't do.** Interactive experiences like Sky Caddy are a differentiator worth surfacing, especially to golf clients.

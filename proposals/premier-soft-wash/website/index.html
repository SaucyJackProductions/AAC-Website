FAQ

Good questions.

What’s the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast dirt off hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning solution that kills mildew and algae at the root, which is safer for siding, stucco, roofs and paint and keeps it clean longer. We use each where it fits.

Is soft washing safe for my plants and pets?

Yes. We wet and rinse the plants and shrubs around the work area and take care with landscaping on every job. We’ll let you know if pets should stay inside while we work.

How often should I wash my house?

In the Lowcountry’s humidity, most homes look their best with a soft wash every year or two. Roofs, decks and driveways vary with shade and tree cover. We’ll recommend a schedule for your property.

Do you clean businesses and restaurants?

Yes. We clean storefronts, patios, dining areas and windows, and offer monthly, quarterly and six-month plans so your business always looks ready for guests.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Premier Soft Wash is licensed and insured for residential and commercial work.

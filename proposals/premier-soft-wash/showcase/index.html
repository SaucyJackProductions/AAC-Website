How Chandler gets access

Every account is created or kept in Premier's name, on Premier's login and card. Above All Media is added as a manager or partner, so access can be removed with one click.

1 · Google Business Profile

a

Check who is the Primary Owner today. If it is a Topline login, Premier's Gmail is made Primary Owner first.

b

On Google, search "Premier Soft Wash", open the profile menu ⋮, then Business Profile settings → Managers → Add, and add Chandler's email as Manager.

2 · Domain (premiersoftwashsc.com)

a

Look up the registrar (ICANN lookup). Confirm the registrant is Premier. Topline's help center says Premier keeps the domain on cancel; if Topline registered it, ask for the transfer code first.

b

Add Chandler with the registrar's delegate access (GoDaddy "Delegate Access", Namecheap "Share Access", Cloudflare "Members"). No password sharing.

3 · Google Ads

a

Ask Topline whether the ad account sits under its manager account. If it does, request admin rights or create a new account on Premier's Google login with Premier's card.

b

Chandler sends a link request from the Above All Media manager account; Brandon accepts it under Admin → Access and security → Managers. Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager work the same way.

4 · Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

a

Make sure the Page, Instagram and ad account sit in a Meta business portfolio owned by Premier.

b

In Business settings → Partners, add Above All Media's business ID as a partner with access to the Page, ad account and Pixel.

Before cancelling Topline: export every customer contact (including the 134 unasked), download all job photos, copy any posts worth keeping, and set redirects from the old page addresses so Google keeps the ranking history. Cancel only after the new site is live and the domain points to it.

Google Workspace CRM: the near-free option

  • Runs on the Google Workspace account Premier already uses
  • Every lead in one Sheet Premier owns, viewable on any phone
  • Instant email and text replies, quote follow-ups, review requests after each job
  • Built and run by Above All Media

The trade-offs, honestly

  • No built-in crew scheduling app, quotes or invoices; use Google Calendar plus Square or QuickBooks invoices
  • Chandler maintains the scripts; if a Google quota or setting changes, we fix it
  • Business texting needs a one-time carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) before texts go out
  • If Premier grows past 2–3 crews, moving to Jobber later is easy, because the data is already in a Sheet

What Premier's CRM keeps on every client

  • Name, phone, email, address and neighborhood
  • Property type (home, condo, HOA, commercial), stories, size, surfaces
  • Every service bought, dates, photos, crew notes (gate codes, pets)
  • Job value, cost and profit per job, payment status
  • How they found Premier and who referred them
  • Review left (yes/no), next service due date
  • Email and text consent, with date

We leave out race, color, ethnicity and similar personal traits: they don't help sell a house wash and can create legal risk.

The Premier Weekly + referrals

  • One email a week: new before-and-after video, a seasonal tip, and one offer
  • Offers rotate: referral "give 15%, get 15%", seasonal bundles, annual-rinse reminders, a monthly coupon
  • Texts kept to 1–2 a month for the best offers, so people don't opt out
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link and Premier's address (CAN-SPAM); texts go only to people who agreed to them in writing (TCPA)
  • Referral codes tracked in the CRM, so Premier sees which customers bring the most work
How it turns into results: real filmed video, a review engine that runs after every job, and ads without a markup. Premier keeps a faster site it owns, one CRM for jobs, and every lead followed up automatically.

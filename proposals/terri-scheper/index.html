Where the cottage stands

Guests already love it. Now the listing has to show it.

Bluffton Cottage on Alljoy has a perfect 5.0 in every category, a Superhost with eleven years and 139 reviews behind her, and a location most hosts would envy: a mile and a half from Old Town and under half a mile from the Alljoy boat ramp. What it doesn't have yet is media that sells the feeling of staying there.
